> why a meta-model

DistilBERT is strong, but not infallible — wrong roughly 1 in 20 times here. Rather than improving the base model, this system predicts when it's about to fail, using signals it can't see itself: cross-model disagreement, confidence calibration, and known hard patterns like negation or intensifier words used as praise.
